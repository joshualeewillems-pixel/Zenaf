# Mediation review checklist

Quick **yes/no** pass on an AI output against the mediation posture in **`docs/MEDIATION_OPERATIONAL_NOTE.md`**. Use on drafts, live responses, or copy destined for users. **Not** for architecture or refactors — **output review only**. If most answers are “no,” the text is weak on mediation even if it sounds confident.

---

## Checklist (~30 seconds)

Answer **Yes / No / N/A** (N/A = question does not apply to this output type).

1. **Multiple framings:** Where the situation could reasonably be read more than one way, did the output **name at least two** plausible readings (or explicitly state why only one applies)?

2. **Interests vs positions:** Did it **separate** what was *said or demanded* from what might be *needed or at stake* (when that distinction matters for the task)?

3. **Real constraints:** Did it call out **constraints** (time, capacity, policy, dependencies, scope) that are **grounded in the supplied context**, not only generic “consider X”?

4. **Assumptions vs facts:** Did it **label or isolate assumptions** instead of blending them with facts from the input?

5. **Inspectable tensions:** Where there is tradeoff or disagreement, did it **leave the tension visible** (e.g. A vs B, costs of each) instead of faking a single merged answer?

6. **No false synthesis:** If tensions stay unresolved, did it **avoid** wording that pretends everyone agrees or that the tradeoff vanished?

7. **Human-owned next step:** Did it propose a **concrete next move** that a **person** can approve, edit, or reject (not “the system decides”)?

8. **No clinical / identity claims:** Did it **avoid** diagnosing people, attributing traits, or identity judgments?

9. **Anchored to context:** Did claims **tie back** to the actual input (quotes, fields, paths, facts) rather than drifting into vague advice?

10. **Clearer path to action:** After reading, is it **easier** to see what to do next than before — even if the answer is “defer until X is known”?

---

## Review standard

A strong mediation output **does not have to resolve everything**. It should **clarify the situation**, **surface real tensions**, and **support a human-owned next move** — including “wait” or “gather evidence” when that is honest.
