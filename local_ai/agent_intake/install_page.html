Send to Zenaf

One tap on any ChatGPT or Claude conversation sends it to your Zenaf intake — sorted into strands, mined for projects. No copy/paste.

iPhone — chat with Muse (recommended)

Open Zenaf → Connect and paste the conversation (or drop a share link). Muse sorts it into your seven strands and pulls out your projects — no setup, no Shortcut.

Your intake is private to your account: it needs your sign-in and your train:self consent (Settings → Identity). Nothing trains until you review and approve it.

Desktop — Browser Extension

Desktop — Browser Extension

Fully automatic: install the Zenaf Renderer extension and it picks up render jobs on its own — no taps per conversation. Source at local_ai/agent_intake/extension/ in the Zenaf repo: open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, Load unpacked, select the folder, then set your Zenaf URL in its Options page.
